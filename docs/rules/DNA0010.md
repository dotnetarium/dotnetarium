# DNA0010: Insecure cookie configuration

CWE-614, CWE-1004, CWE-1275. Explicit cookie settings weaken protection or cause browsers to reject cross-site cookies.

**Fix:** Keep authentication/session cookies `HttpOnly` and require HTTPS with `SecurePolicy=Always`. Choose `SameSite` for the application's cross-site needs; `SameSite=None` requires a secure cookie.

**Scope:** Reports authentication/session `HttpOnly=false`, `SecurePolicy=None`, and `SameSite=None` without `SecurePolicy=Always`. Inline custom cookies are checked only for `SameSite=None` without `Secure=true`. This rule does not establish a CSRF vulnerability.
